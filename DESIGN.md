# DESIGN.md — Material (Material Design 3)

> Machine- and human-readable design language for this app. Coding agents and
> reviewers: read this BEFORE building any UI. It is the source of truth for
> look and feel. Generated by the design system; the theme task may re-tint the
> brand color only.

## Direction
Google's Material You. Roboto type, a confident purple primary with a teal accent, tonal surfaces and true elevation shadows. Familiar and accessible — ideal for productivity and enterprise tools.

**Reference:** build it like Google Material You products.
Match that level of intentionality — not a generic SaaS template.

## Principles
- Material 3 tonal surfaces and true elevation shadows.
- Purple primary is the deliberate Material identity (not slop here).
- Roboto type, generous touch targets, clear state layers.

## Tokens (authored in OKLCH; consume via Tailwind semantic classes)

- Radius base: `0.75rem` (`rounded`, `rounded-sm`, `rounded-lg`)
- Elevation: `shadow-sm` → `shadow-md` (hover) → `shadow-lg`
- Type: headings `'Roboto', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif`, body `'Roboto', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif`
- Density: comfortable

## Components
Compose from the primitive library at `src/components/ui/` — `Button`, `Card`,
`Input`, `Label`, `Badge`, `Icon`, `PageHeader`, `Table`, `Modal`, `EmptyState`,
`Spinner`. Import: `import { Button, Card } from '@/components/ui';`
Icons: `<Icon name="LucideName" />` — never a text character or emoji.

## DO
- Use the semantic tokens for every color. Use `<PageHeader>` for page titles.
- Let content breathe; lead with a real sentence; left-align unless centering is
  deliberate. Give interactive elements hover + focus-visible states.
- Keep one primary action per view; reserve the accent for one thing per screen.

## DON'T (anti-slop)
- ❌ Raw Tailwind palette colors (`bg-blue-600`, `text-gray-700`, `bg-slate-*`).
- ❌ Indigo/violet/purple gradients on a centered hero (the "AI slop" tell).
- ❌ A row of three identical rounded icon-cards as the hero. Use a list/table if
  it is just data.
- ❌ 0.1-opacity drop shadows everywhere, or rounding every corner by reflex.
- ❌ Inter/Roboto as the headline voice here — use the heading font above.
